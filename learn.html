    Quick-Scoring: Part I & II

    General Awareness (20)

    • 15 min/day current affairs (last 6 months)
    • Static GK: PSU/defence/aerospace basics, Constitution, schemes & awards
    • Recall-based — don't over-invest time

    English & Reasoning (40)

    • English: error spotting, synonyms/antonyms, sentence correction, one-word substitution
    • Skip long comprehension unless time allows — low ROI
    • Reasoning: series, coding-decoding, blood relations, syllogisms, direction sense

    High-Weightage Technical Topics (100 marks)

    Formula Shortlist — memorise cold

    • Big-O ladder: 1 < log n < √n < n < n log n < n² < 2ⁿ < n!
    • Round Robin turnaround/waiting time formulas
    • Subnetting: usable hosts = 2^(32−prefix) − 2
    • Average access time = hit time + miss rate × miss penalty
    • Address lines = log₂(memory size); 1K→10, 1M→20, 1G→30
    • Normal forms: 1NF atomic · 2NF no partial · 3NF no transitive · BCNF determinant is a key
