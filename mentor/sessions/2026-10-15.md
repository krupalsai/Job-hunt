## B. One new learning unit — Indian Polity: Parliament and States

### Explained simply

The Constitution gives Parliament, by ordinary law, the power to add or reshape
States. The power comes from three Articles, and each one answers a different
question:

- **Article 2:** Can Parliament admit or establish a new State that is *not yet
  part of* the Union? Yes.
- **Article 3:** Can Parliament form a new State *inside* the Union, or change
  an existing State's area, boundaries or name? Yes, by law.
- **Article 4:** Is a law made under Article 2 or 3 a constitutional
  amendment? No. So it passes by a simple majority, not the special majority
  that Article 368 requires.

### Worked example (step by step)

Question in your head: *"Parliament wants to rename a State. What is needed?"*

1. **Which Article?** Renaming an existing State is an alteration of its name,
   so Article 3 applies.
2. **Does the President do anything first?** Yes. The Article 3 proviso
   requires the bill to be referred to the State legislature, which gives its
   views within a set period.
3. **Do those views bind Parliament?** No. Parliament decides.
4. **Does it need a constitutional amendment?** No. Article 4 says the law is
   not an amendment for the purposes of Article 368.
5. **Answer:** an ordinary law, after the State's views are sought, with no
   veto for the State.

### Facts that MUST LEARN

- Article 2 covers States *not yet in* the Union. Article 3 covers States
  *already in* the Union.
- Article 3 covers formation, change of area or boundaries, and change of name.
- The President must refer an Article 3 bill to the State legislature for its
  views within a specified period. The views are not binding.
- Article 4: laws under Articles 2 and 3 are not amendments under Article 368.
- Telangana became India's 29th State on **2 June 2014**, under the Andhra
  Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014.

## Answer key

### B. Questions for this unit

- **B.1.** B. Article 3
  - Article 3 covers formation of a new State within the Union and changes to an existing State's area, boundaries or name. Article 2 covers admitting or establishing States that are not yet part of the Union.
  - Memory trick: Article 3 = Three changes to a State: form it, reshape it, rename it.
- **B.2.** B. Refer the bill to that State's legislature to express its views within a set period
  - The Article 3 proviso requires the President to refer the bill to the State legislature for its views within a specified period. Those views are not binding; Parliament decides.
  - Memory trick: Refer, don't ask: the State is consulted, not given a veto.
- **B.3.** B. Article 4
  - Article 4 says such laws are not amendments under Article 368. That is why they pass by a simple majority rather than the special majority needed to amend the Constitution.
  - Memory trick: Article 4 = Ordinary, not Amendment: 3 makes it, 4 keeps it ordinary.
- **B.4.** A. 2 June 2014
  - Telangana was formed on 2 June 2014 under the Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014. 2 June is observed as Telangana Formation Day.
  - Memory trick: Twice June: 2 June 2014 for Telangana's birth as a State.
- **B.5.** A. Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014
  - The Andhra Pradesh Reorganisation Act, 2014 provided for the division of Andhra Pradesh and the creation of Telangana. The 1956 Act reorganised states on linguistic lines and is a different law.
  - Memory trick: Telangana's birth certificate is the 2014 Reorganisation Act.
- **B.6.** A. Changing the name of an existing State
  - Renaming a State falls under Article 3, and Article 4 keeps such a law outside Article 368. Fundamental Rights, the Preamble and the President's term need a constitutional amendment.
  - Memory trick: Names, areas, forms: ordinary law. Rights, Preamble, term: amendment.
- **B.7.** A. Article 2
  - Article 2 covers admission or establishment of new States not yet in the Union. Article 3 deals with States already inside the Union, through formation, alteration or renaming.
  - Memory trick: Article 2 = Outside States joining; Article 3 = Inside States reshaped.
